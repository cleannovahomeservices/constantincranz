For more than thirty years, I have practiced and taught the principles of conscious creation — drawing on a synthesis of teachings from the Ascended Master traditions, A Course in Miracles, the Way of Mastery, the Law of Assumption, Walter Russell's cosmology, Advaita Vedanta, Tibetan Bon and Dzogchen, and the broader contemplative lineages.

The work I do is not coaching in the contemporary sense. It is not motivation. It is not problem-solving. It is the conscious art of living from the I AM that you already are — letting identity precede behaviour, letting the inner shape the outer, letting imagination do the creative work it was given to do.

I work with those who are ready to step out of seeking and into sovereign creation. Not beginners. Not those who need to be persuaded. Those whose life has brought them to a moment that requires a different kind of attention than they have been able to give themselves.

I am based in Medellín, Colombia, and work internationally with clients across many cultures and contexts. The Sovereign Year is the deepest container I offer.

The Container

What the Year Holds

A private, year-long container in which a small cohort of twelve practitioners walks directly with me toward the embodied recognition of their own sovereign creation. Not a curriculum that ends. A year that is walked.

Time is part of the container. Six months in, what is happening cannot be made to happen in six weeks. A year, with proper containing, makes possible what shorter forms cannot.

One

Direct, Personal Contact

Substantial individual time with me throughout the year — not a coaching program in the contemporary sense, but a real, ongoing relationship of mentorship. Held with rigor.

Two

The Field of Twelve

The cohort is held at twelve. Small enough that each is fully attended to. Large enough that the field carries. Twice-monthly group sessions held privately, in the company of others walking the same depth.

Three

Movement Through Cycles

The year moves through cycles — establishment, deepening, integration, embodiment. Each cycle has its own architecture. Each cycle moves the work to a different layer than the one before.

Four

An Investment That Matches the Depth

The investment — financial, energetic, temporal — is significant. This is intentional. Practitioners who arrive at this work have generally moved through what shorter, lower-investment forms can deliver, and are ready for something whose weight matches the depth of what is being asked.

Who This Is For

And Who It Is Not

This work is for those who have walked some form of inner work for years, perhaps decades. Those who have read deeply, sat in retreats, worked with teachers, and recognise that what is being offered here is what life is now asking of them.

It is for those who can hold the rigor of a year — financially, temporally, energetically. Those who arrive without needing to be convinced. Those who already know.

It is not for those new to inner work. It is not for those looking for transformation through a single offering. It is not for those who want a lower-cost version of the same. It is not the right container if you find yourself reading this and feeling pressure or sales language — neither of which I have any wish to put on you.

If reading this stirs something — not a wanting, but a recognition — the application below is the next step.

The Door

The Application

The application is not a formality. It is the first conversation. Take your time with it — twenty or thirty minutes is appropriate. Write as you would write to a teacher you respect.

What you share here will be read carefully and personally. If the year is right for you, we will speak. If not, I will say so honestly.

After You Apply

What Happens Next

One

Your application is received.

You receive a brief confirmation email immediately. The application itself will be read by me, personally, within a few days.

Two

You hear back within seven days.

Either I will write to invite a conversation, or I will write honestly to say the year is not the right form for you in this moment. There is no waiting in silence.

Three

If we speak, we speak.

The first conversation is unhurried — typically sixty to ninety minutes. We meet each other, name what is alive, and discover together whether the year is the right container. Investment, structure, and dates are discussed openly.

Four

Both of us decide.

You decide whether this is what life is asking. I decide whether I am the right teacher for what is being asked. Both must say yes. If they do, the year begins.
